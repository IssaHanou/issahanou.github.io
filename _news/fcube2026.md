I participated in the [F+Cube program](https://www.tudelft.nl/ewi/over-de-faculteit/afdelingen/software-technology/f-cube) this week for Future Female+ Faculty. It was a very inspiring week where I got to meet other women who share my ambition to pursue a career in academia. We exchanged ideas and experiences and joined many motivating workshops on grant writing, research vision presentation, job applications, communication skills, and more! I highly recommend the program for anyone interested in an academic career identifying as Female+.
