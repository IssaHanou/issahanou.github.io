Together with Ziyulong Wang and Thomas Heerdink, I organized a joint seminar between our [ROBUST-RAIL Lab](https://www.tudelft.nl/delftrail/projects/robust-rail) and the [Digital Rail Traffic Lab](https://www.tudelft.nl/citg/over-faculteit/afdelingen/transport-planning/research/labs/drtlab) of the Transport & Planning Department in Delft. 
It was very nice to share the research and insights, resulting in fruitful discussion. 
I presented our work on [Replanning with Flexibility](\publications#Hanou2026FlexSIPP) and the current follow-up work we are doing together with Maya Tielman at ProRail.
